7. Key Insights
Add 4 insight cards:

Insight 01 — Choice Overload
Users have too many restaurant options, but many options look similar and do not clearly match their current situation.
Design implication:
Support filtering by mood, occasion, budget, distance, group size, and time.

Insight 02 — Review Noise
Users read reviews but are unsure whether the reviews are real, updated, or relevant to their needs.
Design implication:
Use contextual reviews with tags such as date night, group meal, quick lunch, budget-friendly, quiet place, good for photos.

Insight 03 — Missing Real-Time Context
Users need real-time signals such as opening status, crowd level, waiting time, available seats, and best time to visit.
Design implication:
Add real-time status and practical decision cues.

Insight 04 — Mood-Based Eating
Users often choose food based on mood and social context, not only cuisine category.
Design implication:
Create a mood-based discovery flow such as chill, date, quick meal, comfort food, group hangout, explore something new.

9. User Journey Map
Create a horizontal journey map:
Trigger → Search → Compare → Validate → Decide → Experience

For each stage, include:
User action
User thought
Pain point
Design opportunity

Trigger:
User wants to eat out or plan a meal.
Thought: “Where should we eat today?”
Pain point: No clear starting point.
Opportunity: Mood or occasion-based entry point.

Search:
User checks social media, maps, review apps, or asks friends.
Thought: “There are too many places.”
Pain point: Choice overload.
Opportunity: Smart filters based on context.

Compare:
User opens multiple restaurant options.
Thought: “Which one is actually suitable?”
Pain point: Similar information, hard to compare.
Opportunity: Comparison cards with price, distance, crowd, vibe, rating, and occasion fit.

Validate:
User checks reviews, photos, and opening status.
Thought: “Is this place still good?”
Pain point: Review noise and outdated information.
Opportunity: Contextual and recent review highlights.

Decide:
User chooses a restaurant.
Thought: “I hope this is the right choice.”
Pain point: Low confidence before going.
Opportunity: Decision confidence score or reason-based recommendation.

Experience:
User visits the restaurant.
Thought: “Was this worth it?”
Pain point: Experience may not match expectation.
Opportunity: Post-visit feedback and personalized future recommendations.
