Section 1 — Project Overview:
Create 4 summary cards:
1. Problem
Users who overthink often feel mentally overloaded, struggle to express emotions, and do not know where to start.
2. Goal
Design a low-pressure experience that helps users identify emotions, calm down, reflect, and choose a small next action.
3. Target Users
Young users who experience mild emotional confusion, stress, relationship worries, study pressure, or uncertainty about themselves.
4. My Role
UX Research, Product Thinking, User Flow, Wireframing, UI Design, Interaction Design.

Section 2 — Core UX Problem:
Use a strong statement:
“Most mental wellness apps ask users to explain their feelings before helping them calm down.”
Then explain:
When users are overwhelmed, typing long text can become another burden. The product needs to reduce input effort and guide users step by step.

Vietnamese version:
“Nhiều ứng dụng wellness yêu cầu người dùng giải thích cảm xúc trước khi giúp họ bình tĩnh lại.”
Khi người dùng đang rối, việc phải gõ quá nhiều có thể trở thành một gánh nặng khác. Trải nghiệm cần giảm áp lực nhập liệu và dẫn dắt người dùng từng bước.

Section 3 — Research Insights:
Show 4 insight cards:
Insight 01: Users do not want to explain too much when overwhelmed.
Design response: quick replies, mood buttons, color input, icon selection, and voice option.

Insight 02: Users need guidance before advice.
Design response: check-in → grounding → reflection → AI mirror → small action.

Insight 03: The interface must feel safe and non-judgmental.
Design response: calm colors, short copy, soft motion, gentle tone of voice.

Insight 04: Retention should come from daily ritual, not pressure.
Design response: daily check-in, self-growth journey, mood pattern, gentle streak system.

Section 4 — User Journey:
Create a horizontal journey map with these stages:
Trigger → Check-in → Grounding → Reflection → AI Mirror → Small Action → Growth Ritual

For each stage, show:
- User feeling
- User need
- Design opportunity

Example:
Trigger:
Feeling: overwhelmed, confused, mentally noisy.

Opportunity: open with one gentle question, not a blank input box.
